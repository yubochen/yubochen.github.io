Yubo Chen （陈玉博）

Chen Yubo is a professor and doctoral supervisor at Minzu University of China. He previously served as an Associate Researcher at the Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences. He was selected for the Fifth Young Elite Scientists Sponsorship Program by CAST, the 2022 Global Chinese AI Young Scholar list, the 2022 Youth Innovation Promotion Association of the Chinese Academy of Sciences, and the 2025 Beijing Nova Program. He has been consecutively included in Stanford University’s World’s Top 2% Scientists list in 2023, 2024, 2025, and 2026. His research interests include natural language processing, knowledge graphs, and large models. He has published more than 100 high-level academic papers in venues such as TNNLS, TALSP, ACL, NeurIPS, IJCAI, AAAI, COLING, and ISWC, with over 10,000 Google Scholar citations. One of his papers was selected as an ESI Highly Cited Paper, and two papers were selected in Paper Digest’s lists of the most influential papers at ACL and EMNLP. He has received nine international/domestic paper awards, including the Best Poster Paper Award at ISWC 2023 (CCF-B) and the ACL 2025 Area Chair Recommendation Award (CCF-A). He has published two academic monographs, Knowledge Graph and Knowledge Graph: Algorithms and Practice, which were selected as textbooks under the 13th Five-Year National Key Book Publishing Plan. He has taught the Knowledge Graph course at the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences for many consecutive years, and the course was rated as an Excellent Course of the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences in 2021; he also received the First Prize for Teaching Achievement of the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences. He has led two General Program projects and one Young Scientists Fund project of the National Natural Science Foundation of China, and has served as a core member in the National Natural Science Foundation of China Key Program, the 2030 Next-Generation Artificial Intelligence Major Project, National Key R&D Program projects, and CAS Strategic Priority Research Program projects. The information extraction and knowledge graph construction systems he led and developed have repeatedly won championships and runners-up in international/domestic academic evaluations. He currently/formerly serves as Deputy Director of the Youth Work Committee of the Chinese Information Processing Society of China, Deputy Director of the National Language Resources Monitoring and Research Center for Ethnic Languages, Editorial Board Member of Pattern Recognition, Associate Editor of Data Intelligence, member of the Language and Knowledge Computing Committee of the Chinese Information Processing Society of China, member of the Large Models and Generation Committee of the Chinese Information Processing Society of China, and member of the Information Technology Professional Committee of the China Society for Scientific and Technical Information. He has received the First Prize of the Qian Weichang Chinese Information Processing Science and Technology Award from the Chinese Information Processing Society of China and the First Prize of the Beijing Municipal Science and Technology Progress Award.
